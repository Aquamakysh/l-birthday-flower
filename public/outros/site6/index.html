Whispers of Love

A romantic ode to flowers and hummingbirds

Like hummingbirds that softly sip
From blossoms pink with morning's kiss,
My heart takes flight when you are near,
Drawn to your love so sweet and clear.

The roses blush when you pass by,
The lilies bow their heads so shy,
For none can match your gentle grace,
That paints a smile on nature's face.

As petals dance on zephyr's wing,
And honeybees their tribute bring,
So does my soul in rhythm move,
To melodies of our sweet love.

Let gardens bloom where'er we tread,
With scarlet blooms and gold and red,
For you're the flower, pure and true,
That makes my heart take flight anew.

Forever Yours
