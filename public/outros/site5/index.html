Whispers of Love

A romantic poem of flowers and hummingbirds

Like hummingbirds that softly sip
From petals of the rose's lip,
My heart alights on love so sweet,
Where nectar flows whene'er we meet.

The garden blooms where'er you tread,
A floral path by sunlight fed,
Each blossom turns its face to see
The beauty you bring effortlessly.

As morning glories greet the dawn,
My soul awakes when you are drawn,
To hover near with wings so fleet,
Making every moment complete.

So let us dance 'midst flowers fair,
Two hearts suspended in the air,
Like hummingbird and bloom entwined,
Forevermore our fates combined.

— With love
